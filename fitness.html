Programmes

Four ways to train

Every one of them is programmed, progressed and written down. Nothing here is a random workout generated the night before.

Programme 01

Barbell Strength

Squat, press, deadlift, chin. Linear progression while it lasts, then a proper block structure. Three or four sessions a week, each about seventy minutes.

Best for: anyone who has never been coached through a heavy lift.

Programme 02

Conditioning

Intervals, carries, sled and bike. Built to complement the lifting rather than wreck it. Forty-five minutes, capped at twelve people.

Best for: adding work capacity without losing strength.

Programme 03

Small Group 4:1

Four athletes, one coach, individual programming inside a shared session. The closest thing to personal training that still feels like a team.

Best for: returning from injury, or chasing a specific number.

Programme 04

Open Gym

Full floor access during staffed hours, with a coach available for form checks. Your own programme, our equipment and eyes.

Best for: experienced lifters who already know what they are doing.

Questions

Before day one

I have never lifted before. Am I going to be the worst one there?

Everyone on that floor had a first session. New members spend their first two weeks on an empty bar learning the movement, and the coach is told in advance.

What if I cannot make a class I booked?

Cancel up to two hours before and there is no penalty. The spot releases automatically to whoever is on the waitlist.

Do you work with injuries?

With your clinician’s guidance, yes. Priya runs the return-to-training programme and will happily speak to a physio directly.

Is there a joining fee?

No. No joining fee, no annual fee, no contract. Thirty days’ notice in writing to cancel.

Can I freeze my membership?

Up to eight weeks a year for travel, illness or work. Request it in writing and it starts on the date you choose.
