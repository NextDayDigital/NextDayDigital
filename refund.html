If you haven't received your website within 14 days of payment, you get a full refund.

The website is $499, paid once. It is live within 24 hours of payment, once we have your details. Hosting is included, with no monthly cost.

How to ask

Email anthonyalfano157@gmail.com with the subject "Refund request". Include the name on the payment and the date you paid, so we can find the charge.

Payments are processed by Stripe. We send the refund through Stripe. You do not need an account with us.

Monthly plans

Optional monthly plans are separate from that refund: Care is $29 a month (ongoing small edits, uptime and backup monitoring, security and SSL), Growth is $79 a month, and Pro is $149 a month.

To cancel a monthly plan, email us. You can also use the Manage billing link in the footer. It opens Stripe, where billing for the plan is handled.
