Privacy policy

Effective October 19, 2026

This page says what NextDay Digital collects, how we use it, and how to ask us to delete it.

What we collect

We collect the contact details and site content you send us. That includes your name, business name, email address, phone number, and the material for the website: your logo, photos, hours, services, and the words you want on the pages.

The form on the homepage opens your email app and addresses the message to anthonyalfano157@gmail.com. It does not submit the form to a server we run. Until you send the email, a draft can stay in that browser tab. You can clear the draft on the form. We receive it only when you email it.

Payment

Payments are processed by Stripe. You enter your card on Stripe's pages. We do not store your card number.

Cookies, storage, and other requests

We do not set cookies on this site, and the code does not include an analytics or advertising script.

The site remembers whether you chose light or dark mode. That choice is saved in your browser and is not sent to us.

These pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser requests the font files from Google.

The restaurant demo pages load a public Google Sheet for a sample menu and a sample wine list. The sample menu page may save a copy of that sheet in your browser. Those pages are design demos, and the sheet is not information about you.

How we use it

We use what you send to reply to you, build your website, and deliver the work you paid for.

Selling data

We do not sell your data.

Deletion

Email anthonyalfano157@gmail.com and ask us to delete what we have. We will delete the emails and files we hold. Stripe keeps its own payment records. We do not hold your card number.
