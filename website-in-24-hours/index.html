Questions

Is the $499 site a template?

The themes on this site are starting points for layout. The eight demos are concepts, not client work, and the names and reviews inside them are invented. Your site is built from your logo, photos, hours, services, and service area. It is not a shared template with your name dropped in.

Can I edit the site later?

You get 1 week of free edits after launch. After that week, Care is $29/mo for ongoing small edits, uptime/backup monitoring, security and SSL. Growth is $79/mo. Pro is $149/mo. Cancel monthly plans anytime. You do not have to buy a monthly plan.

What if I already have a website?

Send the link and what you want changed. We will tell you whether to update that site or replace it. The $499 offer is still a site of up to 5 pages, live within 24 hours of payment once we have your details.

Do I need any accounts?

No. No accounts needed. You pay as a guest. We host the site, so you don't need a hosting account. A free .com for the first year is included if needed, and we can transfer it to you later. If you already own a domain, it stays yours.
