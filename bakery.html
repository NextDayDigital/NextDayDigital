Good to know

The usual questions

Why do you sell out so early?

We bake what we can make properly with the oven and the hands we have. Baking more would mean shortening the prove, and that is the one thing we are not willing to trade.

Can I pre-order pastries?

Yes, by four the previous afternoon. In a real build those orders land straight on the kitchen’s morning list with the correct cutoff already applied.

Do you cater for allergies?

We bake with wheat, dairy, eggs and nuts in one small kitchen, so we cannot promise a trace-free environment. Specific requirements are always worth asking about for catering orders.

Is there seating?

Fourteen seats and a bench outside. Weekday mornings are calm; weekends before eleven are not.

Do you deliver?

Catering boxes within five miles. Everything else is collection, because pastry does not survive a long drive.
