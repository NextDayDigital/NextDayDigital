Questions

Short answers.

Will I own the website?

We host the website, so you don't need a hosting account. Your domain stays in your name. A free .com for the first year is included if needed, and we can transfer it to you. If you already own a domain, it stays yours.

How do edits work after launch?

You get 1 week of free edits after launch. After that, optional Care is $29/mo for ongoing small edits, uptime/backup monitoring, security and SSL. Growth is $79/mo and adds Google Business Profile setup and upkeep, basic local SEO, and up to 2 hours of updates a month. Pro is $149/mo and adds 4 Instagram and Google posts a month, and priority same-day changes. Cancel monthly plans anytime.

What happens with the domain?

If you already have a domain, it stays yours. You can add us as a user, or change two DNS records we send you. If you need a domain, a free .com for the first year is included if needed. No accounts needed.

Do I need to open accounts with you or a host?

No. No accounts needed. Payment does not require an account with us, and hosting is included with no monthly cost.

Can you promise the site will rank?

No. We do not promise rankings, traffic, calls, or sales. Basic Google setup means titles, descriptions, and a sitemap. We do not invent reviews or ratings.
