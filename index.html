Before we talk

Questions, answered.

Are the demos real client projects?

No. All eight are fictional design concepts. Every business name, price, address, phone number and review inside them is invented, and the imagery is illustrative. They demonstrate design and structure, not client results.

Can I just use one of the themes as-is?

A theme is a starting point for layout, typography and tone · not a finished site. Your copy, photos, services, colours and any extra pages get built on top of it. Naming the one you liked simply gives the first conversation a direction.

What will my website cost?

$499 one-time buys your website from the pricing section: up to 5 pages, Google setup, live within 24 hours of payment once we have your details, hosting included at no monthly cost, and 1 week of free edits after launch. A free .com for the first year is included if you need one. Optional monthly plans: Care $29 (ongoing small edits, uptime and backup monitoring, security and SSL), Growth $79, and Pro $149. For a bigger custom project, send a short description and you will get a written quote before you decide. Payment is secured by Stripe.

What is your refund policy?

14-day money-back guarantee if you haven't received your website. Refunds are available within 14 days of payment only if we haven't delivered your site. Once your site has been delivered, the $499 is non-refundable.

Do I need to set up any accounts?

No accounts needed. You pay as a guest on Stripe. We host the site, so you don't need a hosting account. Want a free .com? We register and connect it for you, and can transfer it to you later. Already own a domain? Add us as a user, or change two DNS records we send you (about 5 minutes). Your Google Business Profile is optional: Google has the owner verify it, then you can add us as a manager. You can manage a monthly plan anytime from an emailed link, no password.

Does “NextDay” mean it launches tomorrow?

Your site goes live within 24 hours of payment, once we have your details.

What if I do not have good photos?

Most businesses do not at first. Your own phone photos are often better than stock. Send what you have with your details.

Will I own the website?

We host the website, so you don't need a hosting account. Your domain stays in your name. A free .com for the first year is included if you need one, and we can transfer it to you. If you already own a domain, it stays yours.

Who updates it after launch?

You get 1 week of free edits after launch. After that, optional monthly plans cover ongoing help: Care is $29, Growth is $79, and Pro is $149.

Can you work with my existing site?

Yes. Send the link and what you would like to improve, and you will get an honest assessment of whether to update it or rebuild.
